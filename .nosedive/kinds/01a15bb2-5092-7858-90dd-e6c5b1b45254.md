# Dive

A dive is one attempt on a feat or a slice of one, and carries no work of its own: what the work is lives on its feat, so a dive bailed, for a wrong implementation, a diff too big to review or any other reason, orphans nothing (dives-are-attempts). It is not durable, by its Drop when: once it is over and its lessons kept, it can go.

`jump` mints it on its feat, named `<feat name>.<last 6 of its id>`, in the directory of the root that reaches its feat. The feat links it as `dives.feat`, so the tree shows a feat's dives in a section of their own: a dive is a non-durable feat. The dive names its feat in `meta.feat`, as legacy dives do, and links nothing back. Its `scopes` copy the feat's.

- `meta.diver` is who holds it, `<email>#<holder token>`, and null when nobody does.
- `meta.packer` is who last put it down, by git's `user.email`.
- `meta.outcome` is `landed` or `bailed` once it is over, and absent while it is open.

Each loop action (jump, pack, land and bail) that changes a dive appends a section to it, `## <Action> <iso time>`, saying what it did, scope by scope, and commits it to the hero's trunk on origin.

## Drop when

It was jumped and is over, landed or bailed, as `meta.outcome` says; and what its log learned is kept where it lasts: each decision made and each lesson worth keeping recorded on the durable doc it concerns, the feature or area its feat works toward, never only in the dive, whose log goes with it. A bailed dive's reason is such a lesson when the next attempt needs it, as a slice found too big to review. Its feat's `dives.feat` link to it goes first, then `nosedive crud --drop <dive>`.

## Holding

A holder is one checkout of the hero: its holder token is the first 12 hex digits of the sha256 of `<hero top level>/<hero branch>/<email>`, the top level as git prints it. A worktree holds at most one dive. Moving or renaming a hero worktree, or switching its branch, changes its token, so the dives it held look held elsewhere.

The dive on deck is the one dive this worktree holds. pack, land and bail act on it, or on the dive they are given when this worktree holds it; a worktree that somehow holds several is refused, each named with its checkouts.

## Work stays in git

A dive's work is never copied into the hero's trunk before it lands. In each writable scope it is the commits of the dive's local branch, `<hero id>/<hero branch>/<dive id>` in the user's cache (core's hydrate-repo names it), that the scope's work branch on origin lacks. In the hero it is every commit the hero's branch holds that origin's trunk lacks.

pack and bail push that work to `refs/nosedive/<hero id>/dives/<dive id>` on each repo's origin, the hero's included: a ref outside `refs/heads`, so no branch listing shows it and no clone fetches it. It is one ref whichever checkout of the hero packs the dive, so a dive packed from one worktree is jumped from another with its commits; the holder token, not the ref's name, keeps two checkouts from holding it at once. Refs of the dive under an older name, such as `refs/nosedive/dives/<hero id>/<hero branch>/<dive id>`, are still found by a `dives` segment and its id, the name's last part, and the next pack replaces them. A bailed dive's refs stay, for an audit to find by its outcome.

Each scope entry's `ref` holds the value of the dive's ref that the last jump or pack saw, and the hero's sits in an entry keyed by the hero's id, carrying only `ref`. pack and bail push with a lease against it, so a dive's ref never moves under someone who holds it.

Commits a workspace checkout holds that no ref on origin does are never dropped: when jump moves the checkout off them, they are pushed first to `refs/nosedive/<hero id>/orphans/<sha>`, where an audit across repos can find them.

land stamps each commit it publishes with `Nosedive-Dive: <dive id>` and `Nosedive-Feat: <id>`, the dive's first durable feat ancestor, which outlives the dive.

## Ignored files

A scope's ignored files stay out of git. pack, land and bail zip its keepable ones, every ignored file but those under a `node_modules`, `dist` or `build` directory, to the feat's pack, `.nosedive/local/packs/<feat id>/<repo name>.zip`, replacing it, or deleting it when there are none, and leave in place those the checkout still ignores; a bailed dive's `.env` waits there for the feat's next dive. A checkout jump moves from other work has its keepable ignored files zipped to `.nosedive/local/packs/orphans/<repo name>-<time>.zip`, unless they match the repo's newest pack, then removed, and the feat's pack unzipped over it, so a feat never sees another's.

## Canonical kind

A dive is minted as `work:dive`: its `kind` is `dive`, and its `scopes` carry an entry keyed by work's seeded id with `canonical: true` and core's pin of the kind, `version` (the commit that last changed the schema) and `schema` (a digest of it). jump refuses, before anything moves, a schema that is on neither work's origin trunk nor a work branch a feat names for work. A hero that is work itself needs no entry, since the repo holding a dive owns its kind.

Every reader of dives asks core's doc-kind, and takes a doc for a dive only when its owner is work and its name is `dive`: a dive of a hero's own kind of that name is not work's. The entry is never rewritten by the work loop; a scope's `ref` is only the newest dive ref jump or pack saw, and is kept apart from the pin.

## Progress

Divers record progress with `pass --turn <dive> [<summary>] [--next <gist>] [--label <label>]`. The summary may be `-` to read stdin. The dive must be held by this worktree. Each turn appends a timestamped section, with a summary and/or a one-line Next gist, in one ordinary pass commit.

## From a branch

`meta.from` names the hero's local branch a dive is worked against in place of trunk, set when it is jumped `--from` that branch (see jump); a dive without it is worked against trunk.
