# Dive origin

A scope with a work branch whose origin is the hero's own would share its dive ref with the hero, and land its unmerged work on the hero's main as the hero's. So jump, and pack, land and turn before they write, refuse such a scope, naming the scope, the origins compared and the dive's refs on them; bail alone ends the dive, on its record, leaving its refs as they are (own-scope-publishes-nothing-unmerged).

Origins are compared by every read and push URL, through insteadOf and pushInsteadOf, by host and path, ignoring transport, login, port 22 and a trailing `.git`. When either side cannot be read or parsed they count as the same. Before a scope is hydrated, its repo doc's origin is compared.
