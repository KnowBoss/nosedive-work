# The dive on deck: its doc, its feat, and each scope with its repo, checkout, trunk, pin and work branch

Reads `.nosedive/local/dive` in the ship and returns the dive it names, with its feat and its scopes. Each scope carries its repo doc, its checkout (hydrated when it is missing), the repo's trunk, the dive's pin and the work branch, if any. With nothing on deck it refuses, naming `jump`.
