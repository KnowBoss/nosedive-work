# Dive shape

## Usage

The dive kind links this gate `crud.gate` (see core's crud-gates), so a dive made or changed by crud keeps the shape jump gives one it mints: `meta.feat` names its feat, which the kind's schema requires, its scopes are its feat's, and its feat links it `dives.feat`. A dive can so be minted with crud, briefed in its body, and jumped later by anyone.

- **Before**: any edit of a dive that is held, with a `diver` and no `outcome`, is refused: its doc changes only by `pass --turn`, pack, land and bail, whose writes are not crud's, so the dive branch's copy, on which every record is built, holds every turn and the home's copy is changed by nothing but records (held-dive-doc-edits, turns-publish-when); the refusal says to record a turn, or to pack it first. A drop of a held dive is refused as below. A packed dive, with no `diver`, is edited as any other. An edit that changes a dive's name is refused: its feat names it, and its gist says what it is for. A drop of a dive that is not over, with no `outcome` but a `diver` or a `packer`, is refused: jump it and finish it, landing or bailing it; one never jumped, or over, goes, once core finds its feat records it (drops-recorded). A change of `meta.feat` is refused once the dive was jumped, having a `diver`, a `packer` or an `outcome`: its branches and refs are its feat's.
- **After**: a dive minted, or a doc made a dive, gets its feat's repo scopes, each with the feat's work branch, the feat's `dives.feat` link, and its name, `<last 6 hex of its id>.<feat name>`, as jump names a dive, replacing any crud gave it but `<feat name>.<last 6 hex of its id>`, the name dives were minted with before they went hex first, which it keeps (dive-name-hex-first); a dive whose `meta.feat` changed has its scopes replaced by the new feat's, its link moved there, and is renamed after it, hex first (dive-names-derived). Its other scope entries, its kind's canonical one among them, are kept.
