# Dive shape

The dive kind links this gate `crud.gate` (see core's crud-gates), so a dive made or changed by crud keeps the shape jump gives one it mints: `meta.feat` names its feat, which the kind's schema requires, its scopes are its feat's, and its feat links it `dives.feat`. A dive can so be minted with crud, briefed in its body, and jumped later by anyone.

- **Before**: a drop of a dive someone holds, open with a `diver`, is refused: land, pack or bail it first. A change of `meta.feat` is refused once the dive was jumped, having a `diver`, a `packer` or an `outcome`: its branches and refs are its feat's.
- **After**: a dive minted, or a doc made a dive, gets its feat's repo scopes, each with the feat's work branch, and the feat's `dives.feat` link; a dive whose `meta.feat` changed has its scopes replaced by the new feat's, and its link moved there. Its other scope entries, its kind's canonical one among them, are kept.
