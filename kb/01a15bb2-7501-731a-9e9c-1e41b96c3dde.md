# Pack

## Usage

`<dive>`, when given, must be the dive on deck: the one the hero's dive branch names (see dive-on-deck). Refused off a dive branch (loop-returns-home), and when the dive's home is gone: recreate it.

Refused, before anything moves, when a read-only scope has commits origin lacks or uncommitted changes: they have nowhere to go.

Turns live on the dive branch and its dive ref, and reach the home only when land, pack or bail puts the dive down: pack publishes them in its record. Uncommitted changes are not backed up before a pack: a turn pushes only what is committed (see turn), and pack holds the rest in a work-in-progress commit.

Each writable scope whose checkout is on the dive's local branch, and the hero, has its uncommitted changes, untracked files included, committed as `pack(<dive name>): work in progress`, with the nosedive co-author trailer (see commit-message); its bookkeeping commit in the hero reads `pack(<dive name>): <summary>`. Its work is pushed to its ref on origin, `refs/nosedive/<hero id>/dives/<dive id>`, deleting any other ref of the dive. Before anything moves, each ref of the dive on origin must hold the dive's newest ref as the dive doc recorded it at the last jump or pack (see [dive](01a15bb2-5092-7858-90dd-e6c5b1b45254.md)), or older work of the dive; the dive's own ref may also hold a commit the checkout has, its own earlier publish, so a retry goes on, or the sha this dive last left there, which a turn's push leaves and a rebase since does not change: the dive keeps it in the local ref `refs/nosedive-lease/<dive id>` (see turn). Any other refuses the pack, naming it. The record is the dive doc's and the lease ref's, never the local refs that any fetch into a repo's shared cache moves. It publishes the destination ref first, leased against what it just checked, and moves the lease only once that push has succeeded; only after that succeeds does it delete other refs, each under its own lease. With no work, every ref is deleted under lease. Publication or cleanup failures refuse to put the dive down, keep local work recoverable, and can be safely retried after partial updates or deletions. A ref of the dive pushed since then refuses the pack, naming it.

Each such checkout has its ignored files packed for its feat and is left detached at its base, where its branch meets the work branch on origin, else trunk, its local branch deleted; ignored files the base does not ignore are removed, since they would read as untracked work. Nothing under a configured workspace is packed or removed: the checkout's own, as its repo's config names it, or the hero's when it lies inside the checkout, so a hero checked out as a scope keeps its workspace and every checkout in it. The hero is switched back to the dive's home, the branch it was jumped from (see jump), and its dive branch deleted.

It moves `diver` to `packer`, sets each scope's `ref` to what it pushed, appends a `## <timestamp> packed` section with each scope's work and, when the dive recorded turns, `- turns: <n>`, and commits that on the home, pushed to its upstream, fetching and making it again on the new tip when the upstream moved meanwhile (records-on-jumped-branch). A home that holds commits its upstream lacks has them pushed with it, but only when nosedive committed every one (see jump): before anything is pushed, any other is refused, listed, with "push or drop them first", and when the upstream moved the commits are replayed onto it, nosedive still their committer, or refused with the others if that conflicts.

## Turns

A turn is committed on the dive branch and pushed to the dive's ref, never to the home (see turn), so pack is where it is first published to the home (turns-publish-when). The record is built on the dive branch's copy of the dive doc, which holds every turn section, and is the one commit pushed to the home's upstream; the turn commits ride in the hero's ref of the dive, where each turn's push already left them, so a handoff keeps them and pack does not push them twice, and jump leaves them out when it replays that ref (see jump).

Pack refuses, before anything is pushed or moved, when the home's copy of the dive doc changed since the dive began, naming the doc and the commit that changed it: `<doc> on <home> changed since this dive began, by <sha> <subject>, so nothing was put down and every turn is still on <dive branch>: revert that change on <home>, or ask whoever made it to, then run \`nosedive pack\` again`. That guard never throws turns away: the dive branch keeps them. It is judged on a fresh fetch taken just before the first push, and it is the push itself, fast-forward only and leased on the home's tip, that stops an overwrite, with the check above naming the cause first. A home copy that is this dive's own turns already on main, as a dive in flight before turns waited for put-down has, is neither refused nor doubled.

A home that moves after the work and the dive refs are pushed but before the record is, so that the refusal above would find the dive part put down, is not reported as nothing changed: the refusal says the pack is only partly done, names what was published (each repo's dive ref, with its sha) and what was not (the record on the home), says every turn is still on the dive branch and the other change is untouched, and names the command to run again. The home's copy of the dive doc still moved, so the rerun refuses the same way until that change is reverted on the home, and the message says so rather than promising the rerun finishes; once it is reverted, the rerun finishes what is left and overwrites nothing of the other edit. A home that moves without touching the dive doc is planned again from a fresh fetch and finishes in the same run.
