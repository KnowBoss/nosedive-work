# Puts the dive on deck down: its work pushed to dive refs in its repos, its checkouts back at their pins

Puts the dive on deck down, so anyone, on any ship, can jump it again.

Refused, before anything moves, when a read-only scope has commits past its pin or uncommitted changes: they have nowhere to go.

For each writable scope, uncommitted changes, untracked files included, are committed as `pack(<dive name>): work in progress`. Work past the pin is force-pushed to the dive's ref on the repo's remote; with none, that ref is deleted. Then every checkout is put back at its pin.

It moves `diver` to `packer`, appends `## Packed` with each scope's work, and takes the dive off deck.
