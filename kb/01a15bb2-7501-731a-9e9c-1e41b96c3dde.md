# Pack

Puts the dive on deck down, so anyone, on any hero repo, can jump it again.

Refused, before anything moves, when a read-only scope has commits origin lacks or uncommitted changes: they have nowhere to go.

For each writable scope, uncommitted changes, untracked files included, are committed as `pack(<dive name>): work in progress`. The dive's work is force-pushed to its ref on origin, `refs/nosedive/dives/<hero id>/<hero branch>/<dive id>`, and any ref of the dive another hero pushed is deleted; with no work, every ref of the dive is deleted.

Then each scope's ignored files are packed for its feat (see [dive](01a15bb2-5092-7858-90dd-e6c5b1b45254.md)), and each checkout is put away with core's dehydrate-repo, its ignored files discarded once packed. A checkout that cannot be put away, such as one a running program holds open, is named and left in place: its work is safe on origin, and the next jump moves it.

It moves `diver` to `packer`, appends `## Packed` with each scope's work, and takes the dive off deck.
