# Pack

## Usage

`<dive>` names it when this worktree holds several.

Refused, before anything moves, when a read-only scope has commits origin lacks or uncommitted changes: they have nowhere to go.

Each writable scope whose checkout is on the dive's local branch, and the hero, has its uncommitted changes, untracked files included, committed as `feat(<feat name>): pack work in progress on <dive name>`, with the nosedive co-author trailer (see commit-message); its bookkeeping commit in the hero reads `feat(<feat name>): pack <summary>`. Its work is pushed to its ref on origin, `refs/nosedive/<hero id>/dives/<dive id>`, deleting any other ref of the dive, all in one atomic push leased against what the dive's newest ref was when last jumped or packed (see [dive](01a15bb2-5092-7858-90dd-e6c5b1b45254.md)); with no work, every ref of the dive is deleted. A ref of the dive pushed since then refuses the pack, naming it.

Each such checkout has its ignored files packed for its feat and is left detached at its base, where its branch meets the work branch on origin, else trunk, its local branch deleted; ignored files the base does not ignore are removed, since they would read as untracked work. Nothing under a configured workspace is packed or removed: the checkout's own, as its repo's config names it, or the hero's when it lies inside the checkout, so a hero checked out as a scope keeps its workspace and every checkout in it. The hero is reset to origin's trunk.

It moves `diver` to `packer`, sets each scope's `ref` to what it pushed, appends a `## <timestamp> packed` section with each scope's work, and commits that to the hero's trunk on origin, fetching and making it again on the new tip when trunk moved meanwhile.
