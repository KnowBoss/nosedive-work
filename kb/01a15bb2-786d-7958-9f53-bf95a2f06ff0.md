# Land

## Usage

`<dive>`, when given, must be the dive on deck. Refused, before anything is pushed:

- off a dive branch, or when the dive's home is gone: recreate it (loop-returns-home);
- when the home holds commits its upstream lacks that nosedive did not commit, after fetching it: push or drop them first, each listed. Those nosedive committed are replayed onto the upstream if it moved, nosedive still their committer, and pushed with land's record (see jump); a replay that conflicts is refused as the others are;

- while the hero or a checkout has uncommitted changes, naming each: commit them first;
- while a read-only scope has commits origin lacks;
- while a work branch has moved to a commit the scope's HEAD does not contain. The recourse is `pack`, then `jump`, which rebases the work onto the branch.

Turns live on the dive branch and its dive ref, and reach the home only when land, pack or bail puts the dive down: land publishes them in its record and deletes the dive's refs. Uncommitted changes are not backed up, and land refuses them rather than leave them behind.

Each commit it publishes that no branch on origin holds is stamped first with `Nosedive-Dive: <dive id>`, `Nosedive-Feat: <id>`, the dive's first durable feat ancestor (see [dive](01a15bb2-5092-7858-90dd-e6c5b1b45254.md)), and the nosedive co-author trailer (see commit-message), where the commit lacks them. A commit that carries a nosedive co-author already, of any version, from an earlier stamp or a rebase, gets no second; another's co-author does not stand in for it. The hero's record, `land(<dive name>): <summary>`, carries it too.

Each writable scope whose checkout is on the dive's local branch and holds work its work branch lacks is pushed to it, a fast-forward or the branch's creation; any other is unchanged. Then each scope's ignored files are packed for its feat, every ref of the dive is deleted on origin, and each checkout is left detached at what it holds, its local branch deleted. A ref of the dive holding work this dive did not see refuses the land, naming it; the sha this dive last left there, which a turn's push leaves, is its own, rebased since or not (see turn), and its lease ref is dropped with the dive.

The hero's own work, the commits on its dive branch, rides to the dive's home (records-on-jumped-branch): the dive branch's work, its turn commits left out (see turn), is replayed onto the home, so each turn's section sits once, in the record, its commits stamped, the hero switched back to the home, fast-forwarded to them, and its dive branch deleted; then `outcome: landed` set, `diver` cleared and a `## <timestamp> landed` section recording each push are committed on top, and the home is pushed to its upstream, rebased onto it first when it moved meanwhile. So the hero's work for the dive sits between its jump and land records.

## Turns

A turn is committed on the dive branch and pushed to the dive's ref, never to the home (see turn), and land is where it reaches the home (turns-publish-when). The land record is built on the dive branch's copy of the dive doc, which holds every turn section, so the one record commit pushed to the home's upstream carries them all, with `- turns: <n>` in its lines. The dive branch's turn commits, nosedive's own commits changing only the dive doc, are left out of the replay onto the home, so no section is doubled and no empty commit is left, and the turns already on the dive ref are not pushed again: land deletes that ref. Land refuses, before anything is pushed or moved, when the home's copy of the dive doc changed since the dive began, judged on a fresh fetch taken just before the first push: it names the doc and the commit that changed it, says every turn is still on the dive branch, and says to revert that change on the home, or ask whoever made it to, then run `nosedive land` again; nothing is changed and no turn is thrown away. A home copy that is this dive's own turns already on main, from a dive in flight before turns waited for put-down, is neither refused nor doubled.

A home that moves after a work branch is pushed but before the record is, so that the refusal above would find the dive part landed, is not reported as nothing changed: the refusal says land is only partly done, names the work branches it pushed and what is not yet published (the hero's work and the record), says every turn is still on the dive branch and the other change is untouched, and names the command to run again. The home's copy of the dive doc still moved, so the rerun refuses the same way until that change is reverted on the home, and the message says so; once it is, the rerun finishes without stamping the work twice. A home that moves without touching the dive doc is planned again from a fresh fetch and finishes in the same run.

A land rerun after a failed run finds its record already on the home and replays the turns written after the failed run, appending their sections in a commit `land(<dive name>): turns written after the failed land`, with `- turns written after the failed land: <n>` in its notes.
