# Record dive

Every change an action makes to a dive goes through it: the meta it sets (null clears a field), an `## <Action> <iso time>` section appended with the lines it is given, and any other hero docs it is handed, all committed to the hero in one commit, `<action>(<dive name>): <summary>`. Pushing is the action's: each puts its commit on the hero's trunk on origin.
