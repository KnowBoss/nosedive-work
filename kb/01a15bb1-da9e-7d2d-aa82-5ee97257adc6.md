# nosedive-work

## Actions

- [Bail](01a15bb2-76d1-7aa2-b4e5-dcc260c69c00.md): Abandons the dive on deck, recording why and what it held
- [Jump](01a15bb2-736b-7eac-84a7-3389e72d9590.md): Picks a dive up, or starts one on a feat: checks its repos out for it, on its local branch or read-only at trunk
- [Land](01a15bb2-786d-7958-9f53-bf95a2f06ff0.md): Publishes the dive on deck: each repo's work pushed to its feat's work branch
- [Pack](01a15bb2-7501-731a-9e9c-1e41b96c3dde.md): Puts the dive on deck down for anyone, on any hero repo, to jump again: its work pushed to its refs, its ignored files packed for its feat, its checkouts left at their base

## Kinds

- [Dive](../.nosedive/kinds/01a15bb2-5092-7858-90dd-e6c5b1b45254.md): One attempt on a feat, by one diver at a time, its work kept in its repos until it lands

## Lib

- [Dive on deck](01a15bb2-7be9-786f-9188-5a2f0a0fa08e.md): The dive this worktree holds: its doc, its feat, and each scope with its repo, checkout, trunk and work branch
- [Record dive](01a15bb2-7d7c-7225-8e54-d02070189892.md): Writes a dive's changes: meta set, a dated section appended, committed to the hero
- [Run git](01a15bb2-7f14-7154-9b09-7c2e37805132.md): Runs git in a directory and returns whether it worked and what it said
