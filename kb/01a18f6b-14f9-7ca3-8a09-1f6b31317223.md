# Record turn

Finds the passing dive through dive-on-deck, refusing unless it is the dive on deck. Validates a nonempty summary or next, and a next gist without line breaks. Appends the UTC ISO timestamp (until core's hero-zone helper lands), optional label, summary and Next gist through the current pass edit. It writes no files or commits directly. The gate entrypoint reads a stdin summary before calling it. Integration tests exercise it through the real launcher and pass, including one commit per turn.

Once pass has committed, it takes that commit onto the dive's home and pushes it, through `ctx.committed` (see turn, "Reaching the home"): `putTurnOnHome`, in dive-on-deck's file. It replays nosedive's unpushed commits on the home and then the turn commit onto the upstream as fetched, and hands core's commit-plan the push, leased on that fetch, and the home's ref move; on a moved upstream it plans again, up to three tries. A conflict leaves the home as it was, and a push that fails keeps both commits. Neither throws: each is a `warning:` on stderr and the turn exits 0, since it is recorded on the dive branch. A core with no `ctx.committed` leaves the turn on the dive branch, and says so.
