# Bail

Abandons the dive on deck. `--reason` is required.

For each scope, its ignored files are packed for its feat (see [dive](01a15bb2-5092-7858-90dd-e6c5b1b45254.md)), and commits origin lacks are pushed to an orphan ref, so nothing it held is lost. Its uncommitted changes are dropped, untracked files with them, and the checkout is left detached at the dive's base: where its branch meets the work branch on origin, else trunk. Its local branch and every ref of the dive are deleted.

It appends `## Bailed`, with the reason and, per scope, the orphaned commits by sha and subject with the ref holding them, and whether uncommitted changes were dropped. It sets `outcome: bailed`, clears `diver`, and takes the dive off deck.
