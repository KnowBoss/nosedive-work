# Bail

## Usage

`--reason` is required; `<dive>`, when given, must be the dive on deck. Refused off a dive branch, and when the dive's home is gone (loop-returns-home).

It puts the dive down as pack does (see [pack](01a15bb2-7501-731a-9e9c-1e41b96c3dde.md)): uncommitted changes committed as work in progress, the work published to the dive's refs using pack's lease-guarded, publish-before-delete procedure, ignored files packed for its feat, each checkout left detached at its base with its local branch deleted, and the hero switched back to the dive's home, its dive branch deleted. It does not require atomic-push support. A publication or cleanup failure leaves local work recoverable and safe to retry before local branches or checkouts are removed. The refs stay: a bailed dive's refs are what an audit finds of it.

Turns live on the dive branch and its dive ref, and reach the home only when land, pack or bail puts the dive down: bail publishes them in its record. Uncommitted changes are not backed up before a bail: a turn pushes only what is committed (see turn), and bail holds the rest as pack does.

Its bookkeeping commit in the hero reads `bail(<dive name>): <reason>`, and the work in progress it commits is pack's. It appends a `## <timestamp> bailed` section, with the reason and, per scope, the commits it held by sha and subject with the ref holding them, and `- turns: <n>` when the dive recorded turns. It is built on the dive branch's copy of the dive doc, so the one record holds every turn a dive wrote, which are committed on the dive branch and pushed to its dive ref until a put-down (turns-publish-when). The home's copy of the dive doc having changed since the dive began makes bail refuse, as pack does (see pack), naming the doc and commit and saying what to do next; it changes nothing and never throws turns away. A home that moves after the work and the dive refs are pushed is reported as only partly done, as pack reports it, naming what was published and what was not and the command to run again, `nosedive bail --reason <why>`; no turn is thrown away then either. It sets `outcome: bailed`, clears `diver`, sets each scope's `ref`, and commits that on the home, pushed to its upstream (records-on-jumped-branch). The home's commits its upstream lacks are published with it by pack's rule: only when nosedive committed every one, replayed onto a moved upstream, else refused before anything is pushed (see jump).
