# Bail

## Usage

`--reason` is required; `<dive>` names the dive when this worktree holds several.

It puts the dive down as pack does (see [pack](01a15bb2-7501-731a-9e9c-1e41b96c3dde.md)): uncommitted changes committed as work in progress, the work pushed to the dive's refs, ignored files packed for its feat, each checkout left detached at its base with its local branch deleted, and the hero reset to trunk. The refs stay: a bailed dive's refs are what an audit finds of it.

It appends `## Bailed`, with the reason and, per scope, the commits it held by sha and subject with the ref holding them. It sets `outcome: bailed`, clears `diver`, sets each scope's `ref`, and commits that to the hero's trunk on origin.
