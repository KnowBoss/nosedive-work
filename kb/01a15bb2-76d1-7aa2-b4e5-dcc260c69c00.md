# Abandons the dive on deck, recording why and what it held

Abandons the dive on deck. `--reason` is required.

It appends `## Bailed`, with the reason and, per scope, the commits past its pin by sha and subject, and whether uncommitted changes were dropped: the shas restore the work while git still has them. It deletes the dive's refs, sets `outcome: bailed` and clears `diver`, puts every checkout back at its pin, and takes the dive off deck.
